Question:
At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands be at right angles?

Options:
A) 4:05 5/11
B) 4:38 2/11
C) Both A and B
D) 4:21 9/11

Correct Answer:
C) Both A and B

Detailed Solution:
- Hands are at 90° twice between any two hours
- Formula: Time = (2/11) × |30H ± 15| minutes past H
- For 90° at 4: Time = (2/11) × (120 ± 15)
- Time 1 = (2/11) × 105 = 210/11 = 19 1/11 - not matching
- Using standard formula for right angles:
- Time = 5.5M = 30H ± 90
- M = (120+90)/5.5 = 38 2/11 or (120-90)/5.5 = 5 5/11
- Both times: 4:05 5/11 and 4:38 2/11
