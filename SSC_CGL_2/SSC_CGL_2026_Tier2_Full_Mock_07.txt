Question:
A shopkeeper sells two identical articles at ₹480 each.  On one article he makes a profit of 20 % and on the other he incurs a loss of 20 %.  What is his overall percentage gain or loss on these two transactions?

Options:
A) 4 % gain
B) 4 % loss
C) 0 % (no profit, no loss)
D) 8 % loss

Correct Answer:
B) 4 % loss

Detailed Solution:
- Let the two articles have cost prices \(C_1\) and \(C_2\), and both have the same selling price \(S = ₹480\).
- For the first article sold at 20 % profit:
  \[ C_1 = \frac{S}{1 + 0.20} = \frac{480}{1.2} = ₹400. \]
- For the second article sold at 20 % loss:
  \[ C_2 = \frac{S}{1 - 0.20} = \frac{480}{0.8} = ₹600. \]
- Total cost price \(C_1 + C_2 = 400 + 600 = ₹1{,}000\).
- Total selling price \(S_1 + S_2 = 480 + 480 = ₹960\).
- Overall loss = Total cost price − Total selling price = ₹1{,}000 − ₹960 = ₹40.
- Percentage loss = \(\frac{40}{1{,}000} \times 100 = 4 \%\).
- Therefore, the shopkeeper makes an overall 4 % loss.

Question:
A certain sum of money amounts to ₹12,000 in 3 years and to ₹13,500 in 5 years at the same rate of simple interest.  Find the principal.

Options:
A) ₹9,750
B) ₹10,000
C) ₹9,600
D) ₹10,500

Correct Answer:
A) ₹9,750

Detailed Solution:
- Under simple interest, the difference in the amounts for 3 years and 5 years corresponds to the interest for the extra 2 years.
- The amount after 5 years minus the amount after 3 years gives the interest earned for 2 years:
  \[I_{2\,\text{years}} = 13{,}500 - 12{,}000 = 1{,}500.\]
- Hence the interest for 1 year is \(1{,}500/2 = 750\) rupees.
- In 3 years, the total interest is \(750 \times 3 = 2{,}250\) rupees.
- Therefore, the principal = Amount after 3 years − Interest for 3 years
  \[P = 12{,}000 - 2{,}250 = 9{,}750\,\text{rupees}.\]
- Thus, the required principal is ₹9,750.

Question:
The incomes of two persons A and B are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their expenditures are in the ratio 3 : 4.  If each saves ₹4,000 per month, what is A’s monthly income?

Options:
A) ₹20,000
B) ₹24,000
C) ₹28,000
D) ₹32,000

Correct Answer:
A) ₹20,000

Detailed Solution:
- Let A’s income and expenditure be 5x and 3y, respectively; let B’s income and expenditure be 7x and 4y.
- Since both save ₹4,000, we have two equations:
  \[5x - 3y = 4{,}000 \quad \text{and} \quad 7x - 4y = 4{,}000.\]
- Multiply the first equation by 4 and the second by 3 to eliminate \(y\):
  \[20x - 12y = 16{,}000, \quad 21x - 12y = 12{,}000.\]
- Subtract to get \(x = 4{,}000\).
- A’s income = \(5x = 5 \times 4{,}000 = 20{,}000\) rupees.
- Hence, A’s monthly income is ₹20,000.

Question:
Two persons A and B can complete a piece of work together in 6 days.  B and C can complete the same work together in 8 days, and A and C together can finish it in 12 days.  In how many days will A alone finish the work?

Options:
A) 10 days
B) 12 days
C) 14 days
D) 16 days

Correct Answer:
D) 16 days

Detailed Solution:
- Let \(1/a, 1/b, 1/c\) be the daily work rates of A, B and C respectively.
- We are given:
  \[ \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{6}, \quad \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{8}, \quad \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{12}. \]
- Adding the three equations:
  \[ \left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{12}. \]
- Therefore,
  \[ 2\left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{20 + 15 + 10}{120} = \frac{45}{120} = \frac{3}{8}. \]
- Hence \(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{3}{16}\).
- But \(\frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{8}\), so
  \[ \frac{1}{a} = \frac{3}{16} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{3}{16} - \frac{2}{16} = \frac{1}{16}. \]
- Therefore \(a = 16\) days.
- Thus, A alone will finish the work in 16 days.

Question:
A train 120 m long crosses a platform 180 m long in 18 seconds.  What is the speed of the train in kilometres per hour?

Options:
A) 54 km/h
B) 60 km/h
C) 72 km/h
D) 90 km/h

Correct Answer:
B) 60 km/h

Detailed Solution:
- To cross the platform completely, the train must cover its own length plus the length of the platform: \(120 + 180 = 300\) m.
- Time taken = 18 s.  Therefore speed in m/s = \(300/18 = 16\,\tfrac{2}{3}\) m/s.
- To convert to km/h, multiply by \(18/5\):
  \[16\,\tfrac{2}{3} \times \frac{18}{5} = 16.666… \times 3.6 = 60\ \text{km/h}.\]
- Hence, the speed of the train is 60 km/h.

Question:
Triangle ABC has sides of length 5 cm, 6 cm and 7 cm.  Find the radius of its circumcircle.

Options:
A) \(\tfrac{35\sqrt{6}}{24}\) cm
B) \(\tfrac{7\sqrt{6}}{4}\) cm
C) \(\tfrac{35\sqrt{3}}{12}\) cm
D) \(\tfrac{35}{24}\) cm

Correct Answer:
A) \(\tfrac{35\sqrt{6}}{24}\) cm

Detailed Solution:
- For a triangle with sides \(a\), \(b\), \(c\) and area \(\Delta\), the circumradius \(R\) is given by \(R = \frac{abc}{4\Delta}\).
- Here \(a = 5\), \(b = 6\), \(c = 7\) cm.  First compute the semiperimeter \(s = \frac{5 + 6 + 7}{2} = 9\) cm.
- Using Heron’s formula, the area is
  \[ \Delta = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)} = \sqrt{9\times 4\times 3\times 2} = \sqrt{216} = 6\sqrt{6}. \]
- Therefore, the circumradius
  \[R = \frac{5\times 6\times 7}{4\times 6\sqrt{6}} = \frac{210}{24\sqrt{6}} = \frac{35}{4\sqrt{6}} = \frac{35\sqrt{6}}{24}\ \text{cm}.\]
- Hence, the radius of the circumcircle is \(\tfrac{35\sqrt{6}}{24}\) cm.

- Simplifying gives \(7R^2 = 112\), so \(R^2 = 16\) and hence \(R = 4\) cm.
- Thus, the radius of the base of the cone is 4 cm.

Question:
An urn contains 5 red balls and 4 blue balls. Two balls are drawn one after another without replacement. What is the probability that both balls drawn are of the same colour?

Options:
A) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\)
B) \(\dfrac{4}{9}\)
C) \(\dfrac{5}{18}\)
D) \(\dfrac{7}{18}\)

Correct Answer:
B) \(\dfrac{4}{9}\)

Detailed Solution:
- Total number of ways to choose 2 balls from 9 is \(\binom{9}{2} = 36\).
- Favourable cases: either both balls are red or both are blue.
  * Both red: \(\binom{5}{2} = 10\) ways.
  * Both blue: \(\binom{4}{2} = 6\) ways.
- Total favourable ways = 10 + 6 = 16.
- Probability = \(\frac{16}{36} = \frac{4}{9}.\)
- Hence, the probability that both drawn balls are of the same colour is \(\dfrac{4}{9}\).
