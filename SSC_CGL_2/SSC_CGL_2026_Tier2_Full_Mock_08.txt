Question:
If the 9-digit number 89563x87y is divisible by 72, then what is the value of √(7x - 3y)?

Options:
A) 8
B) 6
C) 5
D) 4

Correct Answer:
B) 6

Detailed Solution:
For a number to be divisible by 72, it must be divisible by both 8 and 9.
1. Rule for 8: The last three digits (87y) must be divisible by 8.
   87y / 8:
   800 is divisible. 72 is divisible.
   So 872 and 880 are close.
   872 is divisible by 8 (8 × 109 = 872).
   So y = 2. (Next would be 880, so only y=2 fits for digit 0-9).
   Value of y = 2.

2. Rule for 9: The sum of digits must be divisible by 9.
   Sum = 8 + 9 + 5 + 6 + 3 + x + 8 + 7 + 2
   Sum = 48 + x
   For (48 + x) to be divisible by 9, the next multiple of 9 is 54.
   48 + x = 54 => x = 6.

3. Calculate √(7x - 3y):
   Substitute x = 6 and y = 2.
   √(7*6 - 3*2) = √(42 - 6) = √36 = 6.

Final Answer is 6.

Question:
The price of petrol increases by 20%. A person wants to increase his expenditure by only 8%. By what percentage (correct to one decimal place) should he decrease his consumption?

Options:
A) 11.1%
B) 10.0%
C) 9.6%
D) 12.5%

Correct Answer:
B) 10.0%

Detailed Solution:
Let initial Price (P) = 100 and Consumption (C) = 100.
Initial Expenditure (E) = P × C = 100 × 100 = 10000.

New Price = 120 (20% increase).
New Expenditure = 10000 + 8% of 10000 = 10800.

Let new Consumption be C_new.
120 × C_new = 10800
C_new = 10800 / 120 = 90.

Decrease in consumption = Initial C - New C = 100 - 90 = 10.
Percentage decrease = (10 / 100) × 100 = 10%.
Exact answers match option B.

Question:
A dishonest shopkeeper promises to sell his goods at 44% loss but uses 30% less weight. Find his actual loss percentage.

Options:
A) 20%
B) 25%
C) 15%
D) 10%

Correct Answer:
A) 20%

Detailed Solution:
Let the Cost Price (CP) of 1 gm be Re 1.
Let he have 1000 gm.
CP of 1000 gm = Rs 1000.

1. Selling Price (SP) promise:
   Sells at 44% loss.
   SP of 1000 gm (promised) = 1000 - 44% of 1000 = Rs 560.

2. Weight used:
   Uses 30% less weight, means he gives 700 gm instead of 1000 gm.
   CP of the goods actually given (700 gm) = Rs 700.

3. Actual Calculation:
   CP (actual) = 700.
   SP (actual) = 560.
   Loss = CP - SP = 700 - 560 = 140.
   Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
   Loss % = (140 / 700) × 100
   Loss % = (1/5) × 100 = 20%.

A) Bus
B) Star
C) Ring
D) Mesh

Correct Answer:
B) Star

Detailed Solution:
In Star topology, all nodes are connected individually to a central connection point (Hub/Switch).
