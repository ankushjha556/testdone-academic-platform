Question:
A and B entered into a partnership investing ₹50,000 and ₹70,000 respectively. A is an active partner and receives 10% of the total profit as salary. The remaining profit is shared in ratio of investments. If the total profit is ₹30,000, find the share of A.

Options:
A) ₹13,500
B) ₹14,250
C) ₹15,250
D) ₹16,000

Correct Answer:
B) ₹14,250

Detailed Solution:
Total Profit = 30000.
A's Salary = 10% of 30000 = 3000.
Remaining Profit = 30000 - 3000 = 27000.
Investment Ratio A:B = 5:7.
A's share in Remaining = (5/12) * 27000.
27000 / 12 = 2250.
A's Profit Share = 5 * 2250 = 11250.
Total Income of A = Salary + Profit Share
= 3000 + 11250
= ₹14,250.
