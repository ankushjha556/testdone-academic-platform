Question:
In an election between three candidates A, B, and C, 80% of the registered voters cast their votes. Candidate A got 30% of the cast votes, B got 20% of the remaining cast votes, and C got the rest. If the difference between the votes received by A and C is 12480, find the total number of registered voters.

Options:
A) 45000
B) 60000
C) 54000
D) 40500

Correct Answer:
B) 60000

Detailed Solution:
Let total registered voters = 100x.
Votes cast = 80x.
Votes for A = 30% of 80x = 24x.
Remaining votes = 80x - 24x = 56x.
Votes for B = 20% of 56x = 11.2x.
Votes for C = Remaining = 56x - 11.2x = 44.8x.
Difference between C and A = 44.8x - 24x = 20.8x.
Given, 20.8x = 12480.
x = 12480 / 20.8 = 600.
Total voters = 100x = 60000.

For div by 11, x-5 = 0 => x=5.
Value = x² - y = 5² - 8 = 25 - 8 = 17.

Question:
From the top of a 75 m high tower, the angles of depression of two points P and Q on the same side of the base are φ and θ respectively. Given tan φ = 5/8 and tan θ = 3/4. If Q is closer to the tower than P, find the distance between P and Q.

Options:
A) 10 m
B) 20 m
C) 30 m
D) 40 m

Correct Answer:
B) 20 m

Detailed Solution:
Height h = 75 m.
P is farther (angle φ, smaller tan). Distance d1 = h * cot φ = 75 * (8/5) = 120m.
Q is closer (angle θ, larger tan). Distance d2 = h * cot θ = 75 * (4/3) = 100m.
Distance PQ = 120 - 100 = 20 m.

*Passage 3: Scientific*
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods with the aid of chlorophyll pigments. In plants, photosynthesis generally involves the green pigment chlorophyll and generates oxygen as a byproduct. The process occurs in two stages: light-dependent reactions and light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle). In the light-dependent reactions, which take place in the thylakoid membrane, energy from sunlight is absorbed by chlorophyll and converted into stored chemical energy. In the light-independent reactions, the chemical energy harvested during the light-dependent reactions drives the assembly of sugar molecules from carbon dioxide. This process is crucial for life on Earth as it provides the base for the food chain and regulates the atmospheric oxygen and carbon dioxide levels.
