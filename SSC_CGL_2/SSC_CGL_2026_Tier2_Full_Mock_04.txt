Topic: Linear Races

Question:
In a race of 1000 m, A beats B by 100 m or 10 seconds. Find the time taken by A to complete the race.

Options:
A) 90 seconds
B) 100 seconds
C) 110 seconds
D) 80 seconds

Correct Answer:
A) 90 seconds

Detailed Solution:
A beats B by 100 m. This means when A finishes (1000m), B is at 900m.
A beats B by 10 seconds. This means B takes 10 seconds more than A to finish 1000m.
Also, B is 100m behind when A finishes. B takes 10 seconds to cover that 100m gap.
Speed of B = 100m / 10s = 10 m/s.
Time taken by B to run 1000m = 1000 / 10 = 100 seconds.
Time taken by A = Time of B - 10s = 100 - 10 = 90 seconds.

Find the reflection of the point (4, -3) in the line y = 2.

Options:
A) (4, 7)
B) (4, 5)
C) (4, 1)
D) (-4, -3)

Correct Answer:
A) (4, 7)

Detailed Solution:
Point P(4, -3). Line y = 2 (Horizontal line).
The reflection P'(x', y') will have the same x-coordinate. x' = 4.
Distance of P from line y=2 is |-3 - 2| = |-5| = 5 units.
P' will be 5 units above y=2.
y' = 2 + 5 = 7.
So P'(4, 7).
