Question:
A man invests Rs 4455 in Rs 10 shares quoted at Rs 8.25. If the rate of dividend declared is 12%, calculate his annual income.

Options:
A) Rs 648
B) Rs 650
C) Rs 720
D) Rs 540

Correct Answer:
A) Rs 648

Detailed Solution:
Investment = 4455. MV = 8.25.
Number of shares = 4455 / 8.25 = 540.
Face Value (FV) = 10.
Total FV = 540 × 10 = 5400.
Income = Dividend % of Total FV.
= 12% of 5400.
= (12/100) × 5400 = 12 × 54 = 648.
