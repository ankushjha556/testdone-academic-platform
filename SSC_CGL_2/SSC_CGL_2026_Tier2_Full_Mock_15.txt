Topic: Compound Interest (Installments)
Difficulty Level: Tough

Question:
A sum of Rs 25220 is borrowed and paid back in 3 equal annual installments at 5% per annum compound interest. Find the amount of each installment.

Options:
A) Rs 9261
B) Rs 8820
C) Rs 9000
D) Rs 8400

Correct Answer:
A) Rs 9261

Detailed Solution:
Let the value of each installment be x.
Rate R = 5% = 1/20.
The present value (PV) of the installments must equal the Principal borrowed.
Principal P = x / (1 + R/100) + x / (1 + R/100)² + x / (1 + R/100)³
25220 = x / (21/20) + x / (21/20)² + x / (21/20)³
25220 = x [ 20/21 + (20/21)² + (20/21)³ ]
25220 = x [ 20/21 + 400/441 + 8000/9261 ]
Take LCM = 9261.
25220 = x [ (20×441 + 400×21 + 8000) / 9261 ]
25220 = x [ (8820 + 8400 + 8000) / 9261 ]
25220 = x [ 25220 / 9261 ]
x = 9261.
Thus, each installment is Rs 9261.

Topic: Mensuration 3D (Hexagonal Prism)
Difficulty Level: Tough
Question:
A right prism has a regular hexagonal base of side 6 cm. If the height of the prism is 10 cm, find its Total Surface Area (TSA).

Options:
A) 360 + 108√3
B) 360 + 54√3
C) 300 + 108√3
D) 360 + 72√3

Correct Answer:
A) 360 + 108√3

Detailed Solution:
For a prism, TSA = Lateral Surface Area (LSA) + 2 × Base Area.
Base is a regular hexagon of side a = 6 cm.
Base Area = 6 × (√3/4) × a²
= 6 × (√3/4) × 6²
= 6 × (√3/4) × 36
= 6 × 9√3 = 54√3 cm².
Two Base Areas = 2 × 54√3 = 108√3 cm².
LSA = Perimeter of Base × Height.
Perimeter = 6 × a = 6 × 6 = 36 cm.
Height = 10 cm.
LSA = 36 × 10 = 360 cm².
TSA = 360 + 108√3 cm².

Difficulty Level: Tough
Question:
Find the number of positive integers less than 200 which are co-prime to 200.

Options:
A) 80
B) 100
C) 60
D) 40

Correct Answer:
A) 80

Detailed Solution:
This requires Euler's Totient Function φ(n).
200 = 200.
Prime factorization of 200: 2³ × 5².
φ(n) = n × (1 - 1/p1) × (1 - 1/p2)...
φ(200) = 200 × (1 - 1/2) × (1 - 1/5)
= 200 × (1/2) × (4/5)
= 200 × (4/10)
= 200 × 0.4
= 80.
So, there are 80 numbers less than 200 that are co-prime to it.

Question:
If x + 1/x = 3, find the value of x⁵ + 1/x⁵.

Options:
A) 123
B) 126
C) 119
D) 150

Correct Answer:
A) 123

Detailed Solution:
Given x + 1/x = 3.
We know x² + 1/x² = 3² - 2 = 7.
We know x³ + 1/x³ = 3³ - 3(3) = 27 - 9 = 18.
Now, multiply (x² + 1/x²) and (x³ + 1/x³):
(x² + 1/x²) (x³ + 1/x³) = (x⁵ + 1/x⁵) + (x + 1/x).
7 × 18 = (x⁵ + 1/x⁵) + 3.
126 = (x⁵ + 1/x⁵) + 3.
x⁵ + 1/x⁵ = 126 - 3 = 123.

Options:
A) 70 min
B) 45 min
C) 60 min
D) 75 min

Correct Answer:
A) 70 min

Detailed Solution:

Equate x:
√3(H - 200) = (H + 200)/√3.
3(H - 200) = H + 200.
3H - 600 = H + 200.
2H = 800.
H = 400 m.
