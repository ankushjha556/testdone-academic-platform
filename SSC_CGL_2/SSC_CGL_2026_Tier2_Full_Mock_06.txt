Question:
The monthly incomes of A and B are in the ratio 4 : 5 and their monthly expenditures are in the ratio 2 : 3.  If A saves one‑quarter of his income, then what fraction of A’s monthly saving is B’s monthly saving?

Options:
A) 1⁄2
B) 3⁄5
C) 2⁄3
D) 5⁄8

Correct Answer:
A) 1⁄2

Detailed Solution:
Let the incomes of A and B be 4x and 5x and their expenditures be 2y and 3y respectively.  A’s saving = 4x − 2y.  According to the condition, A saves one‑quarter of his income: 4x − 2y = (1/4) × 4x ⇒ 4x − 2y = x.  Rearranging gives 2y = 4x − x = 3x ⇒ y = 1.5x.  Therefore B’s saving = 5x − 3y = 5x − 3 × 1.5x = 5x − 4.5x = 0.5x.  A’s saving is x, so the required fraction = (0.5x)/x = 1/2.  Thus B’s saving is half of A’s saving.

Question:
Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and pipe B can fill it in 18 hours.  They work together for 4 hours and then pipe C empties the remaining water in 3 hours.  How long will pipe C alone take to empty the full tank?

Options:
A) 6¾ hours
B) 8 hours
C) 9 hours
D) 12 hours

Correct Answer:
A) 6¾ hours

Detailed Solution:
The rate of A = 1/12 tank per hour and the rate of B = 1/18 tank per hour.  Working together their combined rate is 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 of the tank per hour.  In 4 hours they fill 4 × (5/36) = 20/36 = 5/9 of the tank.  The remaining part is 1 − 5/9 = 4/9 of the tank.  Pipe C empties this remaining portion in 3 hours, so C’s emptying rate is (4/9) ÷ 3 = 4/27 of the tank per hour.  Therefore the time taken by C alone to empty a full tank is the reciprocal, 27/4 hours = 6¾ hours.

Question:

C) ₹2,640
D) ₹2,720

Correct Answer:
B) ₹2,520

Detailed Solution:
For annual compounding, the compound interest after 2 years is given by
CI = P[(1 + r)^2 − 1], where P = 12,000 and r = 10% = 0.10.  Thus
CI = 12,000[(1.10)^2 − 1] = 12,000(1.21 − 1) = 12,000 × 0.21 = ₹2,520.  Hence the interest earned in 2 years is ₹2,520.

Question:
A vessel contains 30 litres of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 2 : 1.  Six litres of this mixture is removed and replaced with water.  What is the ratio of milk to water in the new mixture?

A) 8 : 7
B) 7 : 8
C) 10 : 9

Initially the mixture has milk : water = 2 : 1.  Thus milk = 20 litres and water = 10 litres. Six litres of water is then added, making water = 8 + 6 = 14 litres.  The new ratio of milk : water is 16 : 14 = 8 : 7.

Correct Answer:
A) 14⁄33

Detailed Solution:
The total number of ways to choose 2 balls from 12 is 12C2 = 66.  The number of favourable ways to choose 2 balls that are not black = number of ways to choose 2 from the 8 non‑black balls (5 red + 3 white) = 8C2 = 28.  Therefore the required probability is 28/66 = 14/33.

Time to cross completely = total length ÷ relative speed = 250/25 = 10 seconds.
