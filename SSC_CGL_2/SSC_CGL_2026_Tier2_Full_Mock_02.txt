Question:
When a positive integer 'n' is divided by 14, the remainder is 9. What is the remainder when 'n² + 4n + 3' is divided by 14?

Options:
A) 8
B) 10
C) 12
D) 6

Correct Answer:
A) 8

Detailed Solution:
Let n = 14k + 9.
When divided by 14, we can consider the remainder part only.
n ≡ 9 (mod 14).
Expression: n² + 4n + 3
Substitute remainders:
9² + 4(9) + 3
= 81 + 36 + 3
= 120.
Divide 120 by 14:
14 × 8 = 112.
Remainder = 120 - 112 = 8.
Answer is 8.
