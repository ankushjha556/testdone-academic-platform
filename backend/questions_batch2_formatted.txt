### Q1. (SSC CHSL – Analogy)
A sequence of figures shows a square transforming into a pentagon, then a hexagon.  Which of the following shapes should come next in the sequence?

A. Triangle  
B. Heptagon  
C. Octagon  
D. Nonagon

**Answer:** B. Heptagon

**Explanation:** The number of sides increases by one each time: square (4), pentagon (5), hexagon (6).  The next shape will have 7 sides, i.e., a heptagon.

### Q2. (RRB JE – Number Series)
Find the missing number in the series: 5, 9, 17, 33, 65, …

A. 97  
B. 121  
C. 129  
D. 131

**Answer:** D. 131

**Explanation:** Each term is derived by doubling the previous term and then subtracting one or adding patterns. The answer is 131.

### Q3. (UPSC CAPF – Coding–Decoding)

A. ESOJHF  
B. EOSJHF  
C. FHSJEO

### Q7. (RRB JE – Environment)
Which pollutant is not removed by a catalytic converter in automobile exhaust systems?

A. Carbon monoxide  
B. Hydrocarbons  
C. Sulphur dioxide  
D. Nitrogen oxides

**Answer:** C. Sulphur dioxide

**Explanation:** Catalytic converters reduce CO, hydrocarbons and NOx. Sulphur dioxide is controlled by using low-sulphur fuels.

### Q9. (IBPS Clerk – Economics)
