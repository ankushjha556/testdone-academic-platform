### Q154.  (UPSC / CDS – Physics)
A simple pendulum’s time period depends on:

A. Mass of the bob  
B. Length of the string  
C. Amplitude of oscillation  
D. Material of the bob

**Answer:** B  
**Solution:** The time period **T** of a simple pendulum is given by \(T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}\), where **L** is the length and **g** is acceleration due to gravity.  It depends on the **length** and is independent of the mass of the bob (for small amplitudes).

**Solution:** Water boils at **100°C** at standard atmospheric pressure.  (212°F, 373K).

### Q210.  (UPSC / UGC‑NET – Politics)
The **Parliament of India** consists of:

A. Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha  
B. President, Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha  
C. Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha and Prime Minister

### Q211.  (IBPS PO / SBI Clerk – Quantitative Aptitude)
Find the value of **?**:  
\(25\% \text{ of } 400 + 15\% \text{ of } 200 = ?\)

A. 130  
B. 100  
C. 120  
D. 110

**Answer:** A  
**Solution:** \(25\% \text{ of } 400 = 100\).  \(15\% \text{ of } 200 = 30\).  Total = 100 + 30 = **130**.  Option **A**.
