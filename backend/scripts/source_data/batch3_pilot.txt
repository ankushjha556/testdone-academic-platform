Q15001. SSC MTS/Reasoning – Find the next number in the series: 6, 8, 10, 12, ?
    A. 14
    B. 16
    C. 13
    D. 28

Answer: 14
Solution: The given series is an arithmetic sequence with common difference 2. Starting from 6, it increases by 2 each time. Therefore, the next term after 12 is 14.
