Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 20, b = 21.
First find GCD(20, 21) and then apply the formula:
GCD(20, 21) = gcd(20, 21) = 1.
LCM = 20×21 / 1 = 420.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 5 and the product is 4.
The roots are found by solving x² - 5x + 4 = 0.
The roots are 4 and 1. The larger root is 4.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 3867, Rate = 8%, Time = 5 years.
SI = 3867 × 8 × 5 / 100 = 1546.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 8 days and B in 14 days,
then A's rate = 1/8, B's rate = 1/14.
Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/14 = 11/56.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (11/56) = 5 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 15 and Cost Price = 585.
Profit amount = 585 × 15/100 = 87.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 585 + 87 = 672.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [18, 47, 16, 17, 14]. Sum = 112.
Average = 112 / 5 = 22.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1349, ratio of shares A:B = 3:7.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 7 = 10.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/10 × 1349 = 404.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
Cost1 = 43, Cost2 = 80, Mean cost = 63.

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

Q11: Total balls = 6 (Red: 1, Blue: 5).

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
