Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 8, b = 18.
First find GCD(8, 18) and then apply the formula:
GCD(8, 18) = gcd(8, 18) = 2.
LCM = 8×18 / 2 = 72.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 11 and the product is 30.
The roots are found by solving x² - 11x + 30 = 0.
The roots are 6 and 5. The larger root is 6.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 1582, Rate = 12%, Time = 5 years.
SI = 1582 × 12 × 5 / 100 = 949.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 6 days and B in 5 days,
then A's rate = 1/6, B's rate = 1/5.
Combined rate = 1/6 + 1/5 = 11/30.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (11/30) = 2 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 10 and Cost Price = 916.
Profit amount = 916 × 10/100 = 91.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 916 + 91 = 1007.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [17, 20, 42, 18, 14]. Sum = 111.
Average = 111 / 5 = 22.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1531, ratio of shares A:B = 3:7.

A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/10 × 1531 = 459.
