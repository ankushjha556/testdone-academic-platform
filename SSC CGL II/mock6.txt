Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 19, b = 18.
First find GCD(19, 18) and then apply the formula:
GCD(19, 18) = gcd(19, 18) = 1.
LCM = 19×18 / 1 = 342.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 8 and the product is 12.
The roots are found by solving x² - 8x + 12 = 0.
The roots are 6 and 2. The larger root is 6.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 4740, Rate = 8%, Time = 3 years.
SI = 4740 × 8 × 3 / 100 = 1137.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 11 days and B in 5 days,
then A's rate = 1/11, B's rate = 1/5.
Combined rate = 1/11 + 1/5 = 16/55.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (16/55) = 3 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 15 and Cost Price = 288.
Profit amount = 288 × 15/100 = 43.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 288 + 43 = 331.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [16, 47, 23, 34, 22]. Sum = 142.
Average = 142 / 5 = 28.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1926, ratio of shares A:B = 3:3.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 3 = 6.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/6 × 1926 = 963.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
Cost1 = 48, Cost2 = 93, Mean cost = 77.
Ratio = (Cost2 - Mean) : (Mean - Cost1) = 16 : 29.
Reducing the ratio, we get 16 : 29.

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.
Here base = 17, height = 5.
Area = 1/2 × 17 × 5 = 42.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

Q11: Total balls = 7 (Red: 6, Blue: 1).

= 6/7 = 6/7 in simplest form.

Q12: Speed = Distance / Time. Here distance = 173 km and time = 6 hours.

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
Boat speed = 18 km/h, Stream speed = 5 km/h.
