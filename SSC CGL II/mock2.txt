Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 10, b = 14.
First find GCD(10, 14) and then apply the formula:
GCD(10, 14) = gcd(10, 14) = 2.
LCM = 10×14 / 2 = 70.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 6 and the product is 9.
The roots are found by solving x² - 6x + 9 = 0.
The roots are 3 and 3. The larger root is 3.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 1488, Rate = 8%, Time = 3 years.
SI = 1488 × 8 × 3 / 100 = 357.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 12 days and B in 14 days,
then A's rate = 1/12, B's rate = 1/14.
Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/14 = 13/84.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (13/84) = 6 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 20 and Cost Price = 505.
Profit amount = 505 × 20/100 = 101.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 505 + 101 = 606.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [43, 10, 35, 26, 48]. Sum = 162.
Average = 162 / 5 = 32.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1388, ratio of shares A:B = 3:3.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 3 = 6.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/6 × 1388 = 694.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
Cost1 = 43, Cost2 = 76, Mean cost = 67.

Reducing the ratio, we get 3 : 8.

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.

Area = 1/2 × 24 × 11 = 132.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

Q11: Total balls = 6 (Red: 1, Blue: 5).

Q12: Speed = Distance / Time. Here distance = 174 km and time = 5 hours.

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
