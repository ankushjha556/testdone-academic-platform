DETAILED SOLUTIONS

Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 17, b = 5.
First find GCD(17, 5) and then apply the formula:
GCD(17, 5) = gcd(17, 5) = 1.
LCM = 17×5 / 1 = 85.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 9 and the product is 18.
The roots are found by solving x² - 9x + 18 = 0.
The roots are 3 and 6. The larger root is 6.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 2453, Rate = 12%, Time = 1 years.
SI = 2453 × 12 × 1 / 100 = 294.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 4 days and B in 8 days,
then A's rate = 1/4, B's rate = 1/8.
Combined rate = 1/4 + 1/8 = 3/8.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (3/8) = 2 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 20 and Cost Price = 722.
Profit amount = 722 × 20/100 = 144.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 722 + 144 = 866.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [36, 16, 46, 21, 34, 24]. Sum = 177.
Average = 177 / 6 = 29.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1636, ratio of shares A:B = 3:5.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 5 = 8.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/8 × 1636 = 613.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
Cost1 = 29, Cost2 = 60, Mean cost = 38.
Ratio = (Cost2 - Mean) : (Mean - Cost1) = 22 : 9.
Reducing the ratio, we get 22 : 9.

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.

Area = 1/2 × 15 × 17 = 127.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

Q11: Total balls = 7 (Red: 6, Blue: 1).

= 6/7 = 6/7 in simplest form.

Q12: Speed = Distance / Time. Here distance = 143 km and time = 6 hours.
Speed = 143 / 6 = 23 km/h.

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
Boat speed = 18 km/h, Stream speed = 3 km/h.

Q14: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).

GCD(9, 10) = gcd(9, 10) = 1.
