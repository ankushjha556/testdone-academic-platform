Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 4, b = 15.
First find GCD(4, 15) and then apply the formula:
GCD(4, 15) = gcd(4, 15) = 1.
LCM = 4×15 / 1 = 60.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 9 and the product is 14.
The roots are found by solving x² - 9x + 14 = 0.
The roots are 2 and 7. The larger root is 7.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 3450, Rate = 5%, Time = 5 years.
SI = 3450 × 5 × 5 / 100 = 862.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 10 days and B in 7 days,
then A's rate = 1/10, B's rate = 1/7.
Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/7 = 17/70.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (17/70) = 4 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 25 and Cost Price = 310.
Profit amount = 310 × 25/100 = 77.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 310 + 77 = 387.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [48, 14, 36, 40]. Sum = 138.
Average = 138 / 4 = 34.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1606, ratio of shares A:B = 2:5.
Sum of ratio parts = 2 + 5 = 7.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 2/7 × 1606 = 458.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
