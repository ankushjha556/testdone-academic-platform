Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 10, b = 7.
First find GCD(10, 7) and then apply the formula:
GCD(10, 7) = gcd(10, 7) = 1.
LCM = 10×7 / 1 = 70.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 10 and the product is 21.
The roots are found by solving x² - 10x + 21 = 0.
The roots are 3 and 7. The larger root is 7.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 2434, Rate = 12%, Time = 5 years.
SI = 2434 × 12 × 5 / 100 = 1460.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 8 days and B in 9 days,
then A's rate = 1/8, B's rate = 1/9.
Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/9 = 17/72.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (17/72) = 4 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 20 and Cost Price = 278.
Profit amount = 278 × 20/100 = 55.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 278 + 55 = 333.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [47, 34, 32, 38]. Sum = 151.
Average = 151 / 4 = 37.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1842, ratio of shares A:B = 5:6.
Sum of ratio parts = 5 + 6 = 11.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 5/11 × 1842 = 837.

Q8: Using the alligation method:

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.

Area = 1/2 × 23 × 11 = 126.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
