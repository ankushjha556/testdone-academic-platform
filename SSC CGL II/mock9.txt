Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 14, b = 6.
First find GCD(14, 6) and then apply the formula:
GCD(14, 6) = gcd(14, 6) = 2.
LCM = 14×6 / 2 = 42.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 10 and the product is 21.
The roots are found by solving x² - 10x + 21 = 0.
The roots are 7 and 3. The larger root is 7.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 4089, Rate = 12%, Time = 1 years.
SI = 4089 × 12 × 1 / 100 = 490.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 3 days and B in 13 days,
then A's rate = 1/3, B's rate = 1/13.
Combined rate = 1/3 + 1/13 = 16/39.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (16/39) = 2 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 15 and Cost Price = 826.
Profit amount = 826 × 15/100 = 123.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 826 + 123 = 949.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [18, 14, 42, 49]. Sum = 123.
Average = 123 / 4 = 30.

Q7: Total amount = ₹875, ratio of shares A:B = 2:3.
Sum of ratio parts = 2 + 3 = 5.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 2/5 × 875 = 350.

Q8: Using the alligation method:

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
