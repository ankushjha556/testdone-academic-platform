Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 12, b = 25.
First find GCD(12, 25) and then apply the formula:
GCD(12, 25) = gcd(12, 25) = 1.
LCM = 12×25 / 1 = 300.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 15 and the product is 54.
The roots are found by solving x² - 15x + 54 = 0.
The roots are 6 and 9. The larger root is 9.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 2173, Rate = 6%, Time = 2 years.
SI = 2173 × 6 × 2 / 100 = 260.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 3 days and B in 13 days,
then A's rate = 1/3, B's rate = 1/13.
Combined rate = 1/3 + 1/13 = 16/39.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (16/39) = 2 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 20 and Cost Price = 565.
Profit amount = 565 × 20/100 = 113.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 565 + 113 = 678.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [48, 49, 33, 16]. Sum = 146.
Average = 146 / 4 = 36.

Q7: Total amount = ₹632, ratio of shares A:B = 4:4.

A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 4/8 × 632 = 316.
