Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 8, b = 17.
First find GCD(8, 17) and then apply the formula:
GCD(8, 17) = gcd(8, 17) = 1.
LCM = 8×17 / 1 = 136.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 8 and the product is 15.
The roots are found by solving x² - 8x + 15 = 0.
The roots are 3 and 5. The larger root is 5.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 1031, Rate = 6%, Time = 2 years.
SI = 1031 × 6 × 2 / 100 = 123.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 8 days and B in 15 days,
then A's rate = 1/8, B's rate = 1/15.
Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/15 = 23/120.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (23/120) = 5 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 25 and Cost Price = 782.
Profit amount = 782 × 25/100 = 195.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 782 + 195 = 977.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [29, 34, 29, 44]. Sum = 136.
Average = 136 / 4 = 34.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1706, ratio of shares A:B = 3:4.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 4 = 7.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/7 × 1706 = 731.

Q8: Using the alligation method:

Area = 1/2 × 28 × 18 = 252.
