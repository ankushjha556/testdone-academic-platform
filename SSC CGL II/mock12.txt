Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 20, b = 7.
First find GCD(20, 7) and then apply the formula:
GCD(20, 7) = gcd(20, 7) = 1.
LCM = 20×7 / 1 = 140.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 13 and the product is 30.
The roots are found by solving x² - 13x + 30 = 0.
The roots are 3 and 10. The larger root is 10.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 3591, Rate = 8%, Time = 1 years.
SI = 3591 × 8 × 1 / 100 = 287.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 8 days and B in 12 days,
then A's rate = 1/8, B's rate = 1/12.
Combined rate = 1/8 + 1/12 = 5/24.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (5/24) = 4 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 12 and Cost Price = 324.
Profit amount = 324 × 12/100 = 38.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 324 + 38 = 362.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [42, 11, 35, 48, 26]. Sum = 162.
Average = 162 / 5 = 32.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1259, ratio of shares A:B = 2:5.

A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 2/7 × 1259 = 359.
