Q1: LCM is computed using the formula LCM(a,b) = a×b / GCD(a,b).
Here a = 16, b = 24.
First find GCD(16, 24) and then apply the formula:
GCD(16, 24) = gcd(16, 24) = 8.
LCM = 16×24 / 8 = 48.

Q2: Consider the quadratic equation x² - (sum of roots)x + (product of roots) = 0.
Here the sum of roots is 12 and the product is 27.
The roots are found by solving x² - 12x + 27 = 0.
The roots are 9 and 3. The larger root is 9.

Q3: Simple interest (SI) is calculated using SI = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100.
Here Principal = 3112, Rate = 12%, Time = 2 years.
SI = 3112 × 12 × 2 / 100 = 746.

Q4: Let the total work be 1 unit. If A alone can complete it in 10 days and B in 15 days,
then A's rate = 1/10, B's rate = 1/15.
Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6.
Time to finish together = 1 / combined rate = 1 / (1/6) = 6 days.

Q5: Profit = (Profit% × Cost Price)/100. Here Profit% = 25 and Cost Price = 774.
Profit amount = 774 × 25/100 = 193.
Selling Price = Cost Price + Profit = 774 + 193 = 967.

Q6: Average = (Sum of observations) / (Number of observations).
Numbers: [12, 37, 13, 45]. Sum = 107.
Average = 107 / 4 = 26.

Q7: Total amount = ₹1600, ratio of shares A:B = 3:7.
Sum of ratio parts = 3 + 7 = 10.
A's share = (A's ratio part / sum of ratio parts) × total amount
= 3/10 × 1600 = 480.

Q8: Using the alligation method:
Cost1 = 50, Cost2 = 60, Mean cost = 55.
Ratio = (Cost2 - Mean) : (Mean - Cost1) = 5 : 5.
Reducing the ratio, we get 1 : 1.

Q9: Area of a triangle = 1/2 × base × height.
Here base = 10, height = 13.
Area = 1/2 × 10 × 13 = 65.

Q10: For any angle θ, the Pythagorean identity holds: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.
Thus, regardless of θ = 60°, the sum is 1.

Q11: Total balls = 6 (Red: 5, Blue: 1).

= 5/6 = 5/6 in simplest form.

Q12: Speed = Distance / Time. Here distance = 200 km and time = 3 hours.
Speed = 200 / 3 = 66 km/h.

Q13: Speed downstream = speed of boat in still water + speed of current.
Boat speed = 11 km/h, Stream speed = 3 km/h.
